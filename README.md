> **Automation • Aeronautics • Magic • Exploration • Colonies • Space**

**God of Create** is a modpack for **Minecraft 1.21.1 + NeoForge 21.1.235**, built around **Create** and **Create Aeronautics**, expanding into industrial automation, logistics, vehicles, aeronautics, magic, exploration, colonies, combat, world generation, and space exploration.

The project's goal is not simply to add the largest possible number of mods, but to create a long-term world in which different systems can coexist and interact.

---

## 📌 Overview

God of Create combines different gameplay styles into a single experience:

* ⚙️ **Automation & Industry**
* 🚂 **Transportation & Logistics**
* ✈️ **Aircraft & Physics with Aeronautics**
* 🏭 **Machines & Processing**
* 🪄 **Magic & Supernatural Systems**
* 🏘️ **Colony Building & Management**
* ⚔️ **Combat & Adventure**
* 🌍 **Exploration & World Generation**
* 🗺️ **Structures, Dungeons & New Biomes**
* 🌌 **Space Exploration**
* 🎨 **Advanced Graphics**
* 🌄 **Long-Distance Rendering**
* 🧩 **Integrations & Quality-of-Life Systems**

The goal is to provide a world that can be maintained for a very long time, allowing players to build massive factories, logistics networks, cities, railways, aircraft, monumental structures, and eventually expand exploration into other dimensions and into space.

# 🎯 Modpack Philosophy

God of Create was designed as a **permanent-world modpack**.

The intention is for the world not to be created merely to complete a progression and then be discarded. Players should be able to continue expanding the same world indefinitely.

> **Build a world that continues to make sense as it grows.**

The infrastructure created today should be able to serve as the foundation for larger projects tomorrow.

A factory can supply a city.

A city can receive railways.

Railways can connect regions.

Aircraft can connect distant areas.

And space systems can eventually extend infrastructure beyond the planet.

---

The experience was built around several principles:

### 🏗️ Large-Scale Construction

Large factories, cities, railways, bridges, airports, ports, vehicles, industrial structures, and engineering projects are a central part of the experience.

### ⚙️ Automation

Players can transform manual processes into automated systems, primarily using the Create ecosystem and its addons.

### 🌍 Exploration

World generation, structures, caves, dimensions, and new environments encourage continuous exploration.

### ✈️ Infrastructure

The world can evolve from small machines into complete transportation, logistics, and infrastructure systems.

### 🪄 Parallel Systems

Magic, exploration, combat, and technology do not need to be treated as completely separate progression paths. The pack allows different systems to coexist.

### 🌌 Expansion into Space

Progression does not have to end on the surface of the world. The project also includes systems focused on space exploration and construction beyond the planet.

### 🏘️ MineColonies & Companions

The modpack includes a broad variety of colony systems and companions for your journey, including progression systems involving **Wolves, Horses, and Maids**, designed to complement your adventure.

# 🛠️ Installation

### 1. Considerations

This installation method exists for several reasons:

* Total installation compatibility. This installation method allows you to use virtually any launcher of your choice, whether on Windows, Linux, the official Minecraft launcher, or alternative launchers.

---

* This installation method gives users complete control over their installed mods and modpack without platform-specific limitations or potential content restrictions, such as changes to Modrinth's content policies introduced during the development of this README.

---

* **Why there is no native CurseForge and/or Modrinth installation:** Some mods, datapacks, shaders, and resource packs are available exclusively outside CurseForge or Modrinth (and vice versa) due to circumstances completely outside my control or curation. For this reason, I created this completely independent installation method so that players do not have to deal with installation problems or any other "new feature" that platforms may introduce in the future.

---

* You are free to use whichever launcher you prefer. This guide should work with virtually any launcher with only minor adjustments when necessary. The installation guide uses CurseForge exclusively because it is where the majority of users are concentrated.

---

* **Always make a backup of your modpack folder.**

---

### 2. Installation Steps (CURSEFORGE — Standard for a large portion of players)

1. Clone or download this repository.

   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/rafamendes9/God-of-Create.git
   ```

2. Open CurseForge.

3. Click **"Create"**.

4. Select the modpack name as **"God of Create"** or any name you prefer.

5. Select **Minecraft Version — 1.21.1**.

6. Select **Mod Loader — NeoForge**.

7. Select **Mod Loader Version — 21.1.235**.

8. Click **"Create"**.

9. Right-click the newly created profile and select **"Open Folder"**.

10. Place all files contained inside **"God of Create - Modpack"** into the folder you opened previously. Ignore the **"Extra Assets"** folder and `.git`, `.gitattributes`, and `.gitignore` files.

11. The **"Extra Assets"** folder contains content that must be downloaded by following the respective links to mods found on platforms such as CurseForge, Modrinth, etc., due to GitHub limitations.

12. Close the directory and click **"Play"** on the profile created previously in CurseForge.

> **⚠️ Distant Horizons Recommendation**

I recommend disabling **Distant Horizons** before creating your world and enabling it only after you have already played for several minutes.

This is because Distant Horizons has a built-in terrain pre-generation system, which can negatively affect initial world-generation and startup performance the first time the world is loaded.

To disable it:

* Go to **Options**.
* In the upper-left corner, locate the **Distant Horizons** icon next to the FOV settings.
* Click the icon.
* Change all options from **True** to **False**.

After approximately **10–15 minutes of gameplay**, return to these options and enable:

```text
Enable Rendering - True
LOD Chunk Render Distance Radius - 198
Quality Preset - 3
CPU Load - 2
```

Leave all other options set to **False**.

The **Enable Distant Generation** option should only be set to **True** when generating distant LOD terrain that Distant Horizons needs to render.

# ⚠️ Updating the Modpack

To update your modpack to a newer and optimized version, follow this simple procedure:

1. Open your modpack folder.
2. Locate the **SAVE** folder.
3. Copy this folder to another location outside the modpack directory.
4. Completely delete the rest of the modpack folder and follow the installation guide again.
5. Copy the **SAVE** folder back into the modpack directory.

### Important

Always keep a secure backup of your entire modpack before performing any modification.

The purpose of each repository update is to remain **backward-compatible with existing saves** whenever possible.

If an update is released that breaks this compatibility goal, it will be clearly documented and placed in a dedicated branch or separate repository whenever possible.

# 🌄 Distant Horizons

**Distant Horizons** is one of the most important graphical components of the pack.

It adds a **Level of Detail (LOD)** system that allows players to see far beyond the normal chunk render distance.

This is particularly important in God of Create due to the potential scale of its projects:

* cities;
* railways;
* airports;
* bridges;
* factories;
* mountains;
* large structures;
* bases;
* monumental constructions.

### ⚠️ Distant Generation

Distant generation can significantly increase system load, especially in worlds that have not yet been explored.

For this reason, configuration should be adjusted according to the available hardware.

# 🗺️ World Preparation

For a more stable experience in large worlds, it is recommended to pre-generate part of the terrain before exploring very large distances.

### 🧱 Chunky

**Chunky** can be used to pre-generate an area of the world.

Example:

```text
/chunky center

/chunky radius 1000

/chunky start
```

The radius should be adjusted according to the player's needs.

Pre-generating an area can reduce the amount of chunk generation required during normal exploration.

### 🌄 Chunky + Distant Horizons

These two systems serve different purposes.

### Chunky

Pre-generates actual chunks.

### Distant Horizons

Creates and renders LOD representations of very large distances.

They can be used together.

A possible strategy is:

```text
Chunky

   ↓

Pre-generate important area

   ↓

Normal exploration

   ↓

Distant Horizons

   ↓

Long-distance rendering
```

# 🐛 Support & Troubleshooting

## Common Problems

### **Game does not start?**

* Verify that you have **12+ GB of RAM allocated**.
* Confirm that a compatible and updated Java version is installed ( Java 8 / 17/ 21/ 25).
* Check the Java version required by your launcher and the modpack.

### **Create Aeronautics entities / SubLevels are not working or breaking correctly?**

* Disable or remove your claims from the affected chunks using the **Open Claims** mod.

### **Missing item or mod textures?**

Depending on your SSD storage performance, some textures may take time to load correctly in JEI, especially those from **Rechiseled** and **Chipped**.

This can be completely normal because these mods add a very large number of textures to the game.

If the problem persists or does not automatically correct itself after several minutes of gameplay:

> ⚠️ **Warning:** The following procedure may reset any custom modifications you have made to these configuration files, returning them to their default settings.

**Only perform this procedure in rare cases when necessary.**

Go to:

```text
\God of Create\config
```

Delete the following files:

```text
supplementaries-client

supplementaries-common

quark-common

entity_model_features

entity_texture_features
```

Also delete:

```text
All files with "sodium" in their names
```

and:

```text
create-client
```

If the problem still occurs, try changing the versions of **Rechiseled** and **Chipped** to newer or older versions compatible with the modpack.

A known issue with **Rechiseled** is a **COAL** block variant that may not have a texture. This is a known issue with the mod and, at the time of writing, does not have an effective fix.

---

### **Crashes during startup?**

* Consult `crash_assistant_localization_overrides/README.md`.
* Normally, a popup will provide information about the type of error that occurred.
* Check version compatibility if you modified any mods.
* Increase allocated RAM if necessary.

---

### **Poor performance?**

* Reduce the Distant Horizons render distance to **128** or **98**.
* Adjust Distant Horizons **CPU Load** and **LOD Quality** to **2**.
* Disable auto-rendering from Distant Horizons.
* Close background applications.
* Check whether CPU or GPU utilization is reaching 100%.

This can help determine whether the bottleneck is caused primarily by CPU-side calculations and entities or GPU-side rendering and visualization.

# 🐛 Bug Reporting

Before opening a report:

1. Check whether the problem has already been documented.
2. Test the issue on a copy of the world.
3. Check `latest.log`.
4. Provide the versions involved.
5. Describe exactly what happened.

Avoid generic reports such as:

```text
"It doesn't work"
```

Instead, provide something specific:

```text
"After enabling X, block Y stops updating when the structure enters a specific SubLevel."
```

The more specific the report, the easier it is to reproduce and investigate the problem.
